# P6-Peter-Leap-Ch3

Peter · Reading & Writing Chapter 3. A colourful, tap-friendly practice game for Primary 6 English.

Theme: **Problems around us**. Motto: **Care about world problems**.

Vocabulary and grammar questions are Traditional Chinese. Vocabulary answers stay a picture plus the English word. The pictures are cropped book photos. The Help International newsletter, the Kids-Opedia page, and the model paragraphs stay in English. Buttons, help, section instructions, and feedback are Traditional Chinese.

This is a short writing task, not a formal letter. There is no Dear Principal line and no Yours faithfully line to learn.

## Play online

GitHub Pages is not turned on for this repository. That is on purpose: Eri approves Pages first. This project does not turn Pages on by itself.

When Pages is turned on later, use branch `main` and folder `/` (root). The address would be:

https://tradingservant.github.io/P6-Peter-Leap-Ch3/

## Open locally

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

You can also open `index.html` directly in a browser. The game does not need a server or a network connection. Progress (stars) stays in the browser (`localStorage` key `p6-peter-leap-ch3-v1`).

On an iPhone, use Safari. Buttons are large. Add it to the Home Screen if you want an app icon.

Check the answer key without a browser:

```bash
node tests/answers.test.js
```

Teacher samples stay hidden until a student submits the short paragraph, or until **教師模式** is on.

## What is inside

1. **詞彙 Vocabulary** — ten natural disasters from the book photos. Match the Chinese name to the photo, then match an English sentence to pictures A–J. Fill the effect phrases: destroy, damage, wash away, knock down, kill.
2. **文法 Grammar** — since, too, not enough, and too little. Fierce nature notes become since sentences. Linda and Henry’s problems become since sentences. either / neither / shall we appear only as traps.
3. **閱讀 Play** — the shape is the same as a reading station. The text is the Help International newsletter: Hari in Nepal and David in Kenya, then James Evans’s appeal.
4. **閱讀 Book** — Kids-Opedia, Earthquake: why it happens, and what to do indoors, outdoors, and near the sea.
5. **寫作 Write** — a 4–6 sentence paragraph. It needs since, too or too little, and not enough. Three teacher samples unlock after submit.

Stars are saved on this device. Sound is optional and made in the browser (no audio files). Tap a vocabulary card to hear the English word.

## Answer key

Correct answers follow the Chapter 3 spec. Model paragraphs are marked 範例／教師用 inside the writing step.

- Natural disasters are not man-made. Picture match is A drought through J volcanic eruption.
- An earthquake can destroy houses and buildings and damage roads. A tsunami can wash away people and buildings and destroy boats and ships. A typhoon can knock down trees and bring rainstorms / cause landslides / cause floods. A drought can kill plants and animals.
- Worksheet cloze: washed away ; destroyed.
- since = because. too little water. too expensive. not enough warm clothes. Spell weak, not week.
- Newsletter: Hari, Nepal, earthquake, tents. David, Kenya, drought. James Evans asks readers to donate and join the Volunteer Team.
- Kids-Opedia: plates, tsunami, hide under a strong table, move to an open space, move to higher ground. Under Earthquake, the page does not explain what natural disasters are.
- The writing station is a short paragraph, not a letter to the principal.
